Our work

Projects delivered by Shahin Contracting

Real site photos from projects designed, fabricated and installed by our own teams.

Compressed air

Compressed air plant — piping, filtration & dryers

Complete mechanical installation of a plant compressed air system: from the 3D layout to large-bore headers, air receivers, filter vessels and dryer connections.

  • Large-bore carbon steel air headers, painted and pressure tested
  • Air receivers and filter vessels lifted and set on supports
  • Tie-ins to refrigerated air dryers with insulated lines
  • Gear-operated isolation valves, drains and instrumentation points
Pumping & water

Stainless steel booster pump station

Fabrication and installation of a three-pump booster station for a water treatment area, with stainless steel suction and discharge headers.

  • Three vertical multistage pumps set on a steel base frame
  • Stainless steel headers and reducers, TIG welded on site
  • Butterfly, check and gear-operated DN150 valves
  • Pipe supports, alignment and hydro test
Steel structures

Raw-material silo & bucket elevators

Erection of a raw-material storage silo and twin bucket elevator towers with access platforms for a glass manufacturing plant.

  • Silo shell erection and anchoring
  • Bucket elevator casings and steel support structure
  • Top platforms, ladders and safety handrails
  • Connection to the existing conveyor gallery
